# Services and Portfolio Finally Have a Place

I added dedicated services and portfolio pages to the RitesDev website.

That gives the business site a more useful structure. The services page can talk about the work I offer, while the portfolio has room for projects that show what I have been building.

## It changes the feel of the site

Before those pages, the site was mostly an introduction and a way to get in touch. Those are still important, but they don't tell the whole story of a development business.

Adding the pages made me think harder about how to describe the work. I want the details to be specific enough to help someone decide whether to contact me, without making the site feel like a wall of technical terms.
