# Making the Blog Easier to Update

At first, creating a blog post meant editing the files directly. That works, and for a developer it isn't an especially difficult process. But I wanted to make the everyday task of writing a post feel separate from changing the website itself.

So I started building a Blog Post Manager.

## The useful part is the small workflow

The manager gives me a place to create, edit, and delete posts through a simple interface. It handles the information that goes with a post, like its title, date, and description, along with the writing itself.

I can launch the app locally on my Fedora computer, which keeps it close to the way I already work. I don't need to set up a separate online service just to manage my own blog.

## A tool for one real need

This isn't a large publishing platform. It is a small app for a site I control, built around the task I keep repeating.

The manager is already making that task easier. It also got me thinking about other parts of the site that might benefit from having their own editing tools.
