# The Last Stretch Before Launch

As I get closer to launching RitesDev, I keep finding details I wanted to improve.

Some were visual. Some were about how clearly the site explained the services. Others were about the practical side of getting everything online and making sure the different parts worked together.

## Look at it like a visitor

When I work on the site every day, I know where everything is and what I meant by each page. That isn't true for someone who has just arrived.

I tried to step back and ask whether the navigation made sense, whether the pages explained their purpose, and whether it was clear how to contact me. Those checks are simple, but they catch things that are easy to miss while I'm focused on implementation.
