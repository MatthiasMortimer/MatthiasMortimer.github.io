# The Last Stretch Before Launch

As I get closer to launching RitesDev, I keep finding details I want to improve.

Some are visual. Some are about how clearly the site explains the services. Others are about the practical side of getting everything online and making sure the different parts work together.

## Look at it like a visitor

When I work on the site every day, I know where everything is and what I mean by each page. That isn't true for someone who just arrives.

I step back and ask whether the navigation makes sense, whether the pages explain their purpose, and whether it's clear how to contact me. Those checks are simple, but they catch things that are easy to miss while I'm focused on implementation.
