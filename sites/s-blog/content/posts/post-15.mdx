# The Third Version Had to Do More

As I work on the third major version of RitesDev, I know the site needs to do more than introduce me.

It needed to explain the business, show the work, give people a way to contact me, and make the blog feel connected to the rest of the project. That is a lot more than putting a name and a few links on a homepage.

## Being my own client is unusual

I have to make the decisions about what the site should look like and what it should say. There isn't a separate client handing me a finished list of requirements.

That freedom is nice, but it also means I can keep changing direction. The earlier versions help me learn what I don't want. Now I make each choice serve a clear purpose.

## Build toward something real

The goal isn't to add every feature I can think of. I want a website that helps someone understand RitesDev and take a next step.

I'm still working through the details, but the site is starting to feel less like a test project and more like the foundation for an actual business.
