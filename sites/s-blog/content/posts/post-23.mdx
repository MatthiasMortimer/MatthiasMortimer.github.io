# Reworking the Blog Home Page

Even after the blog works, I keep looking at its home page and thinking about how to make it easier to use.

I want the latest posts to have a clear place, while still leaving room for information about the site and a few projects. That means revisiting the layout instead of adding another block and hoping everything fits.

## The layout has to work at different sizes

On a wide screen, the information cards and the middle column can sit beside each other. On a phone, that same arrangement doesn't make sense, so I make the cards sections people can open and close.

I also work on how scrolling behaves. The post and project list should be comfortable to browse without making the whole page feel like it is fighting the reader.

## Small interactions need care

The home page now has more than links and text. It has responsive sections, focus behavior, and gentle reveal effects for selected elements.

I want those details to stay out of the way. The content should still be readable and usable when someone isn't using a mouse or when motion is reduced.

There's more I can tune, but the page feels closer to the experience I had in mind.
